# Recon Game — unassisted finishing playtest

Status: prepared 2026-10-10 under D87. No human sessions conducted yet. Automated paid runs validate mechanics; they do not establish enjoyment or human medal difficulty.

## Session

Invite 3–5 people who have not played. Allow 35–45 minutes. Start a fresh run at normal speed, without Debug. Say: “Build a company that can look after its ecosystem. Play as you normally would and say what you are thinking.” Let the opening explain the rules. Do not explain the best investment route or coach unless the player asks to stop; record any assistance. Ask permission before recording.

Observe without interrupting:

- First minute: do they understand Start game, scan, repair, and the two-minute healthy-time goal?
- First health failure: do they notice why progress stopped and recover without help?
- Purchases: can they predict the scan price and automatic-scan setup price after upgrading? Do they keep an operating reserve?
- Expansion: can they find affected repositories, navigate modules/projects, and discover group purchases? Note repeated clicking and scrolling frustration.
- Final trial: do they understand coverage, the 60-second trial, and any reset reason? Do they avoid unnecessary manual work?
- Finish: do total time and the medal feel fair? Did the optional sound help or distract?

After play, ask: “When were you bored or confused?”, “Which purchase felt useful?”, and “What would you change first?” Avoid suggesting answers.
