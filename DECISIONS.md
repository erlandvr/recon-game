# Recon Game — decisions

Updated: 2026-10-10. Current rules are maintained in [GAME_BRIEF.md](GAME_BRIEF.md).

Earlier decisions are historical; D42–D44 supersede pause, tutorial-gate, no-queue and saving rules wherever those occur below. D51 supersedes automatic repair in D37/D46.

“User direction” means directly requested in the conversation. “Working choice” means selected to make the first build concrete and remains adjustable through feedback. “Tuning hypothesis” needs playtesting.

## Deferred decisions

- D62 and D61 — **Resolved by Phase 5 (D65–D67), 2026-10-10:** health-qualified progress and five scan-efficiency investments are implemented. Prices and recovery pacing remain tuning choices; human playtesting is outstanding.

- D53 — **Implemented under D70, 2026-10-10:** full autonomy victory now has explicit coverage, operation and observed-work criteria. Values still need human playtesting.

- D52 — **Explicitly on hold, 2026-10-10:** paid remediation tiers, discounts and finding bundles remain deferred. D69 automatic remediation performs ordinary individual repairs; it does not implement this upgrade tree.

- D50 — **Future feature candidate, 2026-10-09:** prolonged lack of scanning or unresolved findings should first cause local repository failure, then spread through dependent repositories and onward like a virus until resolved. Record for later; no gameplay implementation now. Escalation timing, warnings, cycles, recovery and the health floor remain open. Treating absent scans as allowing existing problems to worsen, rather than causing damage directly, is a working interpretation to reconcile with D08. Current Identity dependency damage is not this cascading mechanic.
- D47 — **Historical candidate, resolved by D56–D58 on 2026-10-10:** the user proposes later-level targeting of modules containing repositories and projects containing applications, potentially using the former tab area for navigation. The later user clarification selects projects containing modules and modules containing repositories, now implemented with scoped actions and transparent prices. The earlier application layer is not part of this version.
- D45 — **Implemented by Phase 7 / D71, 2026-10-10:** startup lore and milestone mapping are in the live guidance and optional company story. Human playtesting of emotional connection and idle gaps remains outstanding.
- Final security/compliance tuning and a broader catalogue of obligations beyond the Stage 3 working rules.
- Whether later remediation needs verification scans or carries failure risk.
- Prevention effects, automation policy options, and ongoing operating costs.
- Endless-mode growth curves, grouping limits, and terminal failure conditions.
- Final art assets, audio direction, and hosting destination.
