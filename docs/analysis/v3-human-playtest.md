# V3 unassisted player and spectator check

Updated for V3.4, 2026-10-20. **No sessions recorded.** This applies to the cash-target workshop, not the older Long Game playtest.

Invite a few first-time players and at least one spectator. Say only: “Play as you normally would, and tell me what you are thinking.” Allow them to skip the opening text. Do not explain a purchase strategy. Record assistance separately; ask permission before recording people.

Observe:

- Does the explanation-only opening clearly precede gameplay? Can visitors start immediately and explain building, lost income and Recon taking over repairs?
- At a full estate, can they follow a developer between the islands and read Recon bays without mistaking wires or feature cards for actions?
- Do the longer western walks feel like useful breathing room or excessive waiting? Compare a mixed run and a hiring-only run.

- Can they start and make an intentional first purchase? Record active time to first purchase, hesitation and repeated clicks on a queued order.
- At the first failure, do they recognise lost income and automatic repairs? Do they notice developers being occupied rather than building?
- What do they expect hiring, Recon and Plus to do? Do the numbered bays make sense without explanation? Ask after play rather than teaching during it.
- Can they use Help or Pause & plan, distinguish spending from earnings, and explain the 1,200-credit goal? Saving and no-Recon wins are valid choices.
- Do they notice that victory freezes the time? Is Play again easy to find while the party runs? Test a second run and the reduced-motion option.
- Ask the spectator who builds, why growth slows and what Recon changes. Watching a successful animation does not itself demonstrate understanding.

Keep owner feedback, unassisted observation and automated evidence separate. Fix repeated blockers before changing balance based on a single finish time.
